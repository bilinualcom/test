Shapiful: The Next Generation Platform for 3D Printable Labels, Braille, and Custom Designs

The world of 3D printing has rapidly evolved over the last decade. Once considered a niche technology, it’s now accessible to anyone with a printer and an idea. However, one part of the design process still slows people down: creating clean, functional, and customizable 3D labels — especially when you want to 3D print braille or embed text and QR codes into your models.

Shapiful was built to solve exactly that problem. It’s a browser-based platform that empowers designers, makers, educators, and small businesses to create 3D-printable labels, braille signage, and custom STL models in minutes — no CAD software required.

The Problem: Text and Braille in 3D Design

Designing 3D text or braille usually requires advanced modeling tools. Each letter or braille dot must be aligned precisely and scaled correctly to remain legible when printed.

Even for experienced users, this process is time-consuming and error-prone. For newcomers, it can be an overwhelming technical barrier that discourages experimentation.

Shapiful simplifies that process by providing automated design tools that handle spacing, text generation, and braille accuracy. You can focus on what matters most — the message and purpose of your 3D creation.

What Is Shapiful?

Shapiful is a cloud-based 3D modeling platform dedicated to making 3D labeling and customization simple, accessible, and powerful. It’s not a traditional CAD application. Instead, it offers a lightweight, intuitive interface designed for speed and usability.

With Shapiful, you can:

  • Design 3D-printable labels for home, business, or industrial use.
  • 3D print braille signage, educational aids, or accessibility tools with perfect tactile spacing.
  • Add text, QR codes, or graphics to new or existing models.
  • Upload STL files and instantly personalize them with engravings or embossed features.
  • Choose mounting and shape options for flexible placement.
  • Export printer-ready STL files in minutes.

Everything happens directly in your web browser — no installations, plug-ins, or specialized skills required.

Why 3D Print Braille?

The ability to 3D print braille is more than a feature; it’s a step toward accessibility and inclusion. Tactile design empowers people who are visually impaired to read, navigate, and interact with their environment independently.

Traditionally, creating braille signage or models required expensive equipment or precise manual modeling. Shapiful democratizes that process, letting anyone generate accurate braille text for 3D printing instantly.

Applications include:

  • Braille labels for everyday items (containers, switches, tools).
  • Educational materials for inclusive learning environments.
  • Public signage and wayfinding tools with tactile text.
  • Prototyping accessible product designs.

Shapiful makes it simple to bring these ideas to life — directly from your browser to your printer.

Core Features and Capabilities

Shapiful’s functionality extends far beyond labels. It’s a complete toolkit for customized 3D modeling and additive manufacturing preparation.

1. Intuitive Text Customization

Type your text, choose a font, and decide whether to emboss, engrave, or deboss it. Shapiful automatically adjusts depth, spacing, and alignment for print-ready results.

2. STL Import and Personalization

Already have a 3D design? Upload your STL file and overlay custom details, such as your brand name, serial numbers, or QR codes.

3. Braille Generator for 3D Printing

Convert standard text into tactile, accurate braille instantly. Every dot follows standard dimensions for readability once printed.

4. Export-Ready Files

Shapiful automatically generates STL files that can be sent directly to your 3D printer or manufacturing service.

5. Multi-Purpose Design Options

Beyond labels, create custom stamps, boxes, LEGO-compatible parts, board game pieces, or tokens — all with personalized engravings or braille.
